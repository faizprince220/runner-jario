How To Play

🎮 DESKTOP CONTROLS

• A / D or Left / Right Arrow: Run
• Space / W / Up Arrow: Jump (Press again in air for Double-Jump)
• S / Down Arrow: Drop down through wooden/floating platforms
• E / J / Z: Energy Blade Slash Attack
• ESC / P: Pause Game

📱 MOBILE TOUCH CONTROLS

• ◀ / ▶: Move Left & Right
• ▲: Jump & Double-Jump
• ⚔: Attack with Energy Slash

💎 COLLECTIBLES & SECRETS

• Find all 3 Secret Star Shards in every level for 100% completion!
• Hit '?' Mystery Blocks and Wooden Crates for crystals & powerups.
• Activate Crystal Totems to set your respawn checkpoint!
